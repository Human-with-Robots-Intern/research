# Task : 
A high-level goal that often requires multiple steps to complete.

## Task Attributions
- Task : The name of the overall task.
- Location : The room where the task takes place.
- Estimated Time : Total time estimated to complete the task.
- Subtasks : Steps needed to complete the task, each with its own attributes.

# Subtask :
A subtask is a smaller, more specific component of a task. 
It represents a distinct step or stage in the completion of the overall task.

## Subtask Attributes
- Subtask: The specific action to be performed.
- ID : A unique identifier for each subtask.
- Type: The category of the subtask, which indicates whether the task involves direct interaction with the environment ("Interaction") or observing and waiting for a condition to be met ("Monitoring"). This classification helps determine which tasks can be performed in parallel.

- Asset: The object, tool, or appliance involved in carrying out the subtask. This specifies what the robot will interact with to complete the subtask.

- Parameter: The specific items or inputs required for the subtask. These can include ingredients, tools, or other necessary components.

- Precondition: Conditions or states that must be true before the subtask can be initiated. These preconditions ensure that all necessary preparations are complete before the subtask begins.

- Effect:
    - Add: The list of conditions or states that will become true after the subtask is completed. These effects describe the changes made to the environment or system.
    - Del: The list of conditions or states that will no longer be true after the subtask is completed. These indicate what will be removed or undone as a result of the subtask.

- Duration:
    - Type: Indicates whether the duration of the subtask is controllable (can be adjusted) or uncontrollable (fixed time), which helps in scheduling and prioritizing tasks.
    - Interval: The estimated time in minutes that the subtask is expected to take, allowing for efficient planning and execution.

- TemporalConstraints:
    - Type: Specifies the nature of the timing constraint between subtasks, such as "After" (must occur after another subtask) or "Before".
    - Subtask: The name of the related subtask that imposes the constraint. This establishes the dependency or sequence between subtasks.
    - Interval: The time delay or period required between the related subtasks, ensuring appropriate timing for task dependencies.
    - Urgency: A boolean value indicating whether the subtask must start immediately when the timing constraint is met (true), or if it can begin at any time after the constraint is satisfied (false). This helps prioritize tasks that have strict timing requirements.

- Actions : sequence of actions which compose subtask
    - find <obj>
    - open <obj>,
    - close <obj>,
    - switch_on <obj>
    - switch_off <obj>
    - putin <obj> <obj>
    - putback <obj> <obj>
    - grab <obj>
